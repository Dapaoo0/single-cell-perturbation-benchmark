# Data

The original experiment is Norman et al. 2019 CRISPR activation Perturb-seq,
GEO accession `GSE133344` and BioProject `PRJNA551220`.

The benchmark may use a pinned GEARS-processed AnnData artifact as a convenience
representation. That artifact does not replace the original experimental
provenance. Raw and processed matrices are intentionally excluded from Git;
the repository tracks their URLs, byte sizes, SHA256 checksums, schema evidence,
and transformation decisions.
